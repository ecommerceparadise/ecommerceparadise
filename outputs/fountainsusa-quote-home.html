FAQ

Questions We Get Before Every Order

How much does a large outdoor fountain cost?

Across the four brands on this page, in-stock pieces run from about $619 for a small cast stone piece to roughly $32,000 for a self-contained fire and water installation. Most courtyard and entry fountains land between $2,500 and $12,000 before freight. Send your project details and we quote the exact piece, crating and delivery together.

Do you ship to California, Arizona, Texas and Florida?

Yes. Those four states are where most of our fountain projects go, and we quote freight to residential and commercial addresses in all of them. Large tiered and marble pieces ship by freight carrier with liftgate or curbside delivery, which we confirm in writing before the order is placed.

What is the difference between cast stone and marble fountains?

Cast stone is poured and cured in a mould, which keeps cost down and lets a brand like Giannini Garden offer a very broad catalogue in many finishes. Marble is quarried and hand-carved, so each Metropolitan Galleries piece is heavier, more expensive and unique in its veining. Both are built for year-round outdoor use.

Do I need a plumber or an electrician to install a fountain?

Most self-contained fountains recirculate their own water and need only a nearby GFCI outlet, which an electrician can add. Fire and water features and any piece plumbed to a pool need a licensed installer. We tell you which category your piece falls into before you buy, not after it arrives.

How long does delivery take?

In-stock cast stone and marble pieces typically ship within one to three weeks, plus freight transit. Made-to-order finishes and fire and water units can run six to ten weeks. Your quote states the current lead time for the exact configuration you are ordering.

Do you offer trade or contractor pricing?

Yes. Builders, landscape architects, pool contractors and property managers who buy repeatedly get trade pricing. Mention your company on the quote request and we set it up on the first order rather than making you apply first.
