Subject: Your Google Ads is running blind — quick fix on your end

Hi [Name],

Short version: Google Ads stopped receiving your lead data in August 2025, and
it's costing you money every day it stays that way. The fix is on your side and
takes about ten minutes to set up.

**What's happening**

Your Zoho CRM is connected to Google Ads and the connection still works. What
stopped is the data going into it. Between January and July 2025, Zoho sent
3,353 leads back to Google. Since August 2025 it has sent zero.

**Why that matters more than it sounds**

Google Ads doesn't decide which clicks to buy based on your budget. It learns
from which clicks turned into real leads, then goes and finds more people like
them. That feedback loop is how the account got a $4 cost per lead on your best
keywords.

Right now Google has no idea which of your clicks became customers. So it is
guessing. It keeps bidding on searches that look plausible instead of searches
that actually work, and it has been doing that for over a year. Every day
without lead data, the bidding gets a little further from reality.

**What you need to do**

Every inquiry needs to land in Zoho as a Lead or Contact record. That's it.
The connection back to Google is automatic once the record exists.

1. **Website form submissions** — these are the important ones. When someone
   fills in a form on the site, Zoho captures a hidden Google click ID along
   with their details. That ID is what lets Google match the lead back to the
   click that produced it. If the lead never gets created in Zoho, the match
   never happens.

2. **Phone and email inquiries** — create a Lead record for these too. They
   won't carry a click ID so Google can't attribute them, but you'll want them
   in the CRM anyway for follow-up and for the revenue reporting below.

3. **Update lead status as it progresses** — qualified, quoted, won, lost.
   This is what eventually lets us tell Google not just "this click became a
   lead" but "this click became a $12,000 order", which is where the real
   gains are.

**What to expect**

Data starts flowing back within about 24 hours of the first leads being logged.
Google's bidding needs roughly two to four weeks of it before performance
noticeably improves, so the sooner it starts, the sooner the account recovers.

**One thing to check**

Confirm the website form is still writing into Zoho. If the form was changed,
rebuilt or moved at any point in 2025, that connection may have broken and
would explain the timing. If leads are arriving by email instead of appearing
in Zoho, that's the culprit and we'll get it reconnected.
